This dataset contains partial directed coherence (PDC) and imaginary coherence computed per seizure for 75 patients with drug-resistant epilepsy evaluated at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.

The citation for the associated manuscript is listed below:

Makhoul, G. S., Doss, D. J., Johnson, G. W., Withers, C. P., Cavender, A. C., Hidalgo Monroy Lerma, B., Reda, A., Bibro, C. E., Liao, E., Kang, H., Dawant, B. M., Reddy, S. B., Crudele, A. N., Constantinidis, C., Roberson, S. W., Bick, S. K., Morgan, V. L., & Englot, D. J. (2025). Collapse of interictal suppressive networks permits seizure spread. Brain : a journal of neurology, awaf215. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaf215
